Chenhao Ding 丁晨浩

I am a first-year Ph.D. student in the Department of Creative Informatics, Graduate School of Information Science and Technology, at The University of Tokyo. I am advised by Prof. Hideki Nakayama.

My research interests lie in vision-language models, vision-and-language navigation, and test-time adaptation. I am particularly interested in enabling multimodal models to generalize and adapt to changing environments.

Before joining the University of Tokyo, I received my M.S. in Computer Science and Technology from Xi'an Jiaotong University, advised by Prof. Yihong Gong, and my B.S. in Artificial Intelligence from Xidian University. My doctoral research is supported by the SPRING GX program.
